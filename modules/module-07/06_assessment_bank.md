# Module 7 Assessment Bank (Production Ready — Full Q001–Q060)

## Assessment Blueprint
- **Total Question Pool:** 60 Questions (`M7-Q001` .. `M7-Q060`)
- **Mapping by Learning Objective:**
  - `LO-M7-01` (SPC Implementation Roadmap, CTQ Selection, Rational Subgrouping & Containment Math): `M7-Q001` – `M7-Q015` (15 Questions)
  - `LO-M7-02` (SPC Across Machining, Assembly/Electronics, Injection Molding & Stamping): `M7-Q016` – `M7-Q030` (15 Questions)
  - `LO-M7-03` (Real-Time Monitoring, Actionable Reaction Plans & 3-Tier Escalation SLA): `M7-Q031` – `M7-Q045` (15 Questions)
  - `LO-M7-04` (Layered Process Audit CQI-8, Closed-Loop Control Plan & Workshop 7): `M7-Q046` – `M7-Q060` (15 Questions)
- **Final Exam (`EXAM-M7`):** Random stratified draw of **20 Questions** (`5 L1 + 5 L2 + 5 L3 + 5 L4`)
- **Pass Score:** `80%` (`>= 16 / 20`)
